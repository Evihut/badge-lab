# Galaxy Brain

Awarded for accepted answers in GitHub Discussions.

## Tiers
| Tier | Accepted answers |
|---|---|
| Bronze | 2 |
| Silver | 8 |
| Gold | 16 |

## Notes
- The discussion must be in a repository with Discussions enabled and must use
  an answerable category (Q&A).
- Marking your own answer as accepted in your own repository has historically
  counted, but this is the badge most likely to be tightened.
